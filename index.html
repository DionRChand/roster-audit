How this works

Each week, drop in the full PAY SHEET REPORT export — only rows for Goodman Fielder Footscray are picked out automatically, and hours are summed per Employee ID (a casual can appear on multiple rows). You confirm the Week Ending (WE) date before it's added, so the same paysheet can never silently get logged under the wrong week. Each week's hours are added on top of the running total. How this works

This keeps a running registry of every known Client + Position combination and its Job Code. Drop in the weekly Roster Schedule export and every Client/Position on it gets checked against the registry: an entirely new client, or an existing client rostered under a position it's never used before, gets flagged below and needs a Job Code before it settles. Once a Job Code is entered it's saved to the registry and won't be flagged again. The registry starts pre-loaded from the Week-on-Week Position Tracker.

How this works

Each client's Risk Assessment Date is entered manually — click the date cell to set or change it. The Due Date (12 months later) and Days Remaining are calculated automatically, and the row colour tells you how urgent it is: grey = no date on file, red = overdue, and shades from red through amber to green as the due date gets further away. Tick T Drive Check once that client's folder/paperwork has been confirmed on the T drive. Remarks and everything else save automatically as you edit. Click Mark inactive on a client (e.g. one who has left, or is otherwise on hold) to stop them being tracked — they're greyed out, removed from the totals/overdue counts, and hidden from the default view. Find them again under the Inactive filter, and click Reactivate to bring them back into tracking.
